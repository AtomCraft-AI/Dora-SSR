# Pattern: Map & Timeline Exploration

## Use When

Spatial and chronological relationships are central to understanding.

## Core Loop

Navigate time/place → toggle layers → inspect event/source/data → compare regions or periods → infer pattern/cause/constraint.

## Design Rules

- Keep legend, date/period, and layer state visible.
- Link map locations to event/source cards.
- Support scrub/step through time where change matters.
- Allow comparison of two dates/regions/layers.
- Avoid overcrowding; reveal detail progressively.

## Good Uses

Historical campaigns/trade/urbanization, plate tectonics, climate, population, migration, literary journeys.

## Completion

Annotated map/timeline, evidence-backed spatial explanation, or correctly reconstructed sequence.
