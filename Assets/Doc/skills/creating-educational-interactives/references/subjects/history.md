# History

## Learning Actions

Locate events in time/space, compare sources, distinguish cause from trigger, evaluate evidence, reconstruct perspectives, trace continuity/change, and reason about constrained alternatives.

## Strong Patterns

- [Map & timeline exploration](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/map-timeline-exploration.md)
- [Evidence analysis](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/evidence-analysis.md)
- [System simulation](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/system-simulation.md) only for clearly labeled models

## Design Rules

- Separate **historical fact/source evidence**, **historian interpretation**, and **counterfactual simulation**.
- Show uncertainty where sources are disputed or incomplete.
- Counterfactual branches must be labeled hypothetical and should teach constraints, not claim to reproduce “what would really happen.”
- Connect chronology to geography, institutions, resources, ideas, and actors rather than reducing history to dates.

## Teacher Mode

Provide source reveal, map layers, timeline scrubber, compare-perspectives view, and discussion prompts.

## Avoid

Presentism without context, single-cause explanations for complex events, ahistorical anachronisms, or “choose the correct ideology” persuasion mechanics.
