# Art

## Learning Actions

Observe, compare, compose, manipulate formal elements, create, revise, annotate choices, and analyze visual evidence/context.

## Strong Patterns

- [Creation studio](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/creation-studio.md)
- [Evidence analysis](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/evidence-analysis.md)
- [Interactive visualization](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/interactive-visualization.md) for color/perspective/composition concepts

## Design Rules

- Make formal elements manipulable: line, shape, value, color, texture, space, balance, contrast, rhythm.
- For art history/appreciation, separate visual observation from contextual interpretation.
- Critique should explain effects and choices, not rank a learner's taste.
- Support iteration and comparison of versions.

## Completion Evidence

Created composition plus a short rationale, annotated comparison, or visual analysis grounded in observable features.

## Avoid

Single “correct” composition, style imitation presented as creativity, or scoring personal expression without clear task criteria.
