# Music

## Learning Actions

Listen, identify patterns, manipulate rhythm/pitch/timbre/form, perform sequences, compose, compare versions, and connect notation to sound.

## Strong Patterns

- [Creation studio](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/creation-studio.md)
- [Interactive visualization](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/interactive-visualization.md)
- [Inquiry learning](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/inquiry-learning.md)

## Design Rules

- Synchronize audible events with notation/timeline visualization.
- Let learners change one musical element and immediately hear the effect.
- Use grids or blocks for rhythm/melody where appropriate, but preserve beat and timing relationships.
- For theory, connect symbol → sound → function rather than drill symbols alone.

## Completion Evidence

A performed/composed pattern, corrected rhythm, identified structure, or explanation of how a musical change altered the result.

## Avoid

Visual rhythm indicators that drift out of sync, treating subjective preference as factual correctness, or reward mechanics unrelated to listening/creating.
