# Technology & Engineering

## Learning Actions

Define a need, assemble/build a model, test under constraints, measure performance, identify failure, iterate, and justify trade-offs.

## Strong Patterns

- [Creation studio](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/creation-studio.md)
- [System simulation](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/system-simulation.md)
- [Virtual lab](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/virtual-lab.md)

## Design Rules

- Give explicit constraints: cost, mass, size, strength, energy, reliability, time, materials.
- Test the learner's design against visible criteria.
- Failures should reveal mechanisms, not merely reduce a score.
- Encourage iteration: build → test → inspect → modify → retest.
- Preserve real engineering relationships within the chosen model; label simplifications.

## Good Tasks

Bridge/load design, gear ratios, simple robotics, sensor logic, energy systems, mechanical linkages, circuits, manufacturing/process optimization.

## Avoid

One “correct” aesthetic solution to an engineering problem, arbitrary scoring, or decorative parts that have no functional effect.
