# Civics, Politics & Ethics

## Learning Actions

Understand institutions and rules, analyze cases and sources, identify stakeholders, compare rights/responsibilities, evaluate arguments, and reason through value conflicts.

## Strong Patterns

- [Evidence analysis](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/evidence-analysis.md)
- [Dialogue role-play](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/dialogue-roleplay.md)
- [System simulation](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/system-simulation.md) for institutional/process models

## Design Rules

- Align terminology to the requested curriculum/jurisdiction when specified.
- Distinguish factual institutional description from normative argument.
- For controversial issues, represent relevant perspectives fairly and require evidence/reasons.
- Use stakeholder role-play to expose trade-offs, not to manipulate learners toward a partisan conclusion.
- When rules/laws may vary by time/place, avoid unsupported certainty.

## Completion Evidence

A good outcome is a reasoned decision, claim-evidence map, process reconstruction, or comparison of stakeholder positions.

## Avoid

Partisan scoring, ideological loyalty tests, caricaturing opposing views, or presenting contested normative claims as settled facts.
