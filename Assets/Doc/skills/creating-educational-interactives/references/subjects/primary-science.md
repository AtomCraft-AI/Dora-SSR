# Primary Science

**Use for:** elementary integrated science: matter, force, light, sound, plants, animals, Earth/space, simple engineering and observation.

## Learning Actions

Observe → predict → manipulate one factor → compare → describe evidence. Keep variables concrete and visible.

## Strong Patterns

- [Virtual lab](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/virtual-lab.md) for safe experiments
- [System simulation](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/system-simulation.md) for ecosystems/weather/simple machines
- [Inquiry learning](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/inquiry-learning.md) for prediction and comparison

## Design Rules

- Introduce one main causal variable at a time.
- Use familiar objects and plain language before formal terminology.
- Make measurement visible: ruler, timer, thermometer, count, simple chart.
- Let learners sort/classify living things or materials by observable properties.

## Common Mistakes to Address

“heavier always falls faster,” “plants get food directly from soil,” confusing shadow with reflection, treating seasons as Earth being much closer/farther from the Sun.

## Avoid

Dense dashboards, too many simultaneous controls, or fantastical behavior that contradicts the scientific relationship being taught.
