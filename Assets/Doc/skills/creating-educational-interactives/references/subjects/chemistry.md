# Chemistry

## Learning Actions

Choose apparatus, set conditions, perform procedures, observe macroscopic evidence, connect to particle/chemical representations, control variables, record data, and analyze error.

## Strong Patterns

- [Virtual lab](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/virtual-lab.md)
- [Inquiry learning](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/inquiry-learning.md)
- [System simulation](@agent-skill/builtin/creating-educational-interactives/patterns/system-simulation.md) for particle-level models/equilibrium

## Design Rules

- Distinguish physical handling, observed phenomenon, and chemical explanation.
- Make quantities and units explicit where calculation matters.
- Allow recoverable procedural mistakes and explain their effect on the result.
- In controlled experiments, flag when multiple variables change at once.
- For microscopic views, mark them as models; do not imply particles are literally colored balls at macroscopic scale.

## Teacher Controls

Pause reaction, compare vessels, reveal particle view, show/hide equation, reset trial, vary one parameter.

## Avoid

Unsafe procedural encouragement outside the virtual environment, magic color changes unrelated to chemistry, or using spectacular effects where the chemistry is inaccurate.
